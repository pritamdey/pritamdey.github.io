%----- Profile -----
\section*{Bio}
\SectionRule

I am a statistician working on developing statistical models motivated by real-world scientific problems. My current work spans methodological advances in spatial transcriptomics (statistical theory and methods), variational inference and symbolic regression, as well as application-driven research in multi-omic data integration and neuroscience. I aim to design efficient, robust, and trustworthy statistical models that enable and accelerate scientific discovery.
